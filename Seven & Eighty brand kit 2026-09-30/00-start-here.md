# Seven & Eighty brand kit

The logo is **7°&80°**, with NORTH under the 7 and EAST under the 80. The name comes from 7°N 80°E: Negombo (7.21°N 79.84°E) and Colombo (6.93°N 79.85°E) both round to it.

Every image comes as a PNG to use and an SVG to edit or print. All letters are vector shapes, so nothing needs the fonts installed.

## Wallpapers

The two thin lines crossing behind the logo are the 7°N latitude and the 80°E longitude.

- **iPhone:** send `iphone-wallpaper-1320x2868.png` to the phone. Go to Settings, Wallpaper, Add New Wallpaper, Photos, pick it, and keep it at full size. It is made for the iPhone 16 and 17 Pro Max screen and fits every other iPhone. The logo sits below the clock.
- **Mac:** go to System Settings, Wallpaper, Add Photo, and choose Fill Screen. Use the 5120 × 2880 file on an iMac or Studio Display and the 3456 × 2234 file on a MacBook Pro.

## Email signature

Insert `seven-and-eighty-animated-logo.gif` and set its width to 300 px. It opens on the full logo, then the two degree marks blink off and back on. Outlook for Windows shows only the first frame, which is the full logo. If an email tool refuses GIFs, use `email-logo.png`.

## Fonts

All free on Google Fonts.

- Numbers and headlines: **Bodoni Moda**, 96 pt optical size, Medium. The ampersand is Bodoni Moda Italic.
- NORTH, EAST and small labels: **Familjen Grotesk** SemiBold, all capitals, widely spaced.
- Tab icon only: Bodoni Moda at 6 pt optical size, ExtraBold, so its thin strokes survive at 16 px.

## Rules

- Keep empty space around the logo at least as tall as the 7.
- Main logo: at least 160 px wide on screen or 35 mm in print. Below that, use the horizontal logo or the icon.
- The browser tab icon is "7°" alone, because the full logo cannot be read at 16 to 32 px.
- Do not stretch, outline, add shadows to or recolour the logo outside black and lagoon green.
